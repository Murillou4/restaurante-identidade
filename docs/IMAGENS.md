# Miniaturas do caderno

As miniaturas foram criadas com o ImageGen integrado e publicadas em WebP.

## Compras

[Atlas de compras](../site/public/itens/compras.webp)

```text
Use case: product-mockup. Create one square, photographic food thumbnail atlas for a Brazilian home-kitchen budgeting app, exactly 5 columns by 5 rows of equal square cells, edge to edge with no gutters, frames, lettering, panels or gridlines. Every cell has the same flat light warm ivory backdrop, centered subjects with generous padding on all sides, natural window light, clean restrained overhead studio food photography. Each cell shows ONLY the following clearly identifiable subject, strictly in order left to right:
Row 1: whole raw russet potatoes; raw chicken breast fillets; uncooked streaky bacon strips; a Brazilian smoked calabresa sausage with slices; pieces of cured carne de sol.
Row 2: grated pale mozzarella; small white bowl of creamy Brazilian requeijão; small white bowl of orange creamy cheddar; whole yellow onions; whole garlic bulb and cloves.
Row 3: small white bowl with tomato sauce; small transparent unlabeled bottle of golden cooking oil; small mound of white salt in a bowl; yellow butter pats; small white bowl of pouring cream.
Row 4: yellow corn kernels; fresh broccoli florets; sliced button mushrooms; thin crunchy shoestring potatoes; bunch of chopped parsley and scallion.
Row 5: chopped small pink ham cubes with a slice of ham; small white bowl with dried oregano; lidded rectangular aluminum takeout tray, plain and unlabeled; a plain brown kraft takeout bag; folded white napkins and one disposable fork.
No people, hands, logos, letters, brands, watermark, labels, drawings, strong shadows, background scenes or props. The alignment has to be a strict five-by-five matrix suitable for CSS sprite cropping. Do not merge cells.
```

## Sabores

[Atlas dos sabores](../site/public/itens/sabores.webp)

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural. Make one square food thumbnail atlas for four Brazilian stuffed potato flavor choices. Strict 2-by-2 matrix of equal square tiles, no gridlines, seams or gutters, each centered with ample margin against the same flat warm-ivory tabletop backdrop. Realistic modest home cooking, softly lit overhead three-quarter view with a completely opened large potato skin, creamy smashed potato, and the named topping. The tiles in reading order must be: top left PIZZA flavored potato with visible diced pink ham, melted ivory mozzarella and flecks of dried oregano; top right BACON potato with visible crisp dark diced bacon, melted mozzarella and a thin drizzle of vivid yellow cheddar; bottom left CALABRESA potato with finely grated smoky reddish Brazilian calabresa and melted cheese; bottom right FRANGO potato topped with strands of cooked shredded chicken and a little melted cheese. The potatoes are individually identifiable at tiny size. No text, labels, people, watermark, signage, or decorative props.
```
